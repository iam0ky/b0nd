Interest calculation

"Net rounded up" keeps gross and TDS in paise and rounds the amount credited up to the next rupee, as many brokers do. Most Indian NCDs and bonds use Actual/Actual: each payout = face value × rate × days in the period ÷ 365, or ÷ 366 if the period includes 29 Feb. "Equal payouts" splits the yearly coupon evenly.
